# FinShield
## 1.	Introduction 

Bankruptcy is one of the most critical financial risks faced by businesses, investors, and financial institutions. The ability to accurately predict the likelihood of bankruptcy is essential for minimizing financial losses, ensuring economic stability, and supporting informed decision-making. Traditional methods of bankruptcy prediction, such as ratio analysis and statistical models, often fall short in capturing complex, non-linear relationships within financial data.
Finshield (Bankruptcy Prediction System) is an intelligent solution built using Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) techniques to address this challenge. By leveraging historical financial records, market indicators, and company performance data, Finshield applies advanced predictive models to classify firms as financially healthy or at risk of bankruptcy.
The system aims to provide stakeholders—such as banks, investors, regulatory bodies, and businesses—with an early warning mechanism. This not only helps in risk mitigation but also enables proactive decision-making, resource optimization, and improved financial planning. With AI-driven predictions, Finshield enhances accuracy, scalability, and adaptability compared to conventional approaches.
By combining data-driven insights with the power of modern learning algorithms, Finshield represents a step forward in financial risk management and predictive analytics.

## 2.	Project Objective 

The primary objective of Finshield is to develop a robust and accurate bankruptcy prediction system that can:<br>
**1. Analyze Financial Data:** Collect and analyze financial data from various sources, including company reports, financial statements, and market data.<br>
**2. Identify Patterns and Anomalies:** Utilize machine learning algorithms to identify patterns and anomalies in the financial data that may indicate potential bankruptcy risks.<br>
**3. Predict Bankruptcy Likelihood:** Develop a predictive model that can accurately forecast the likelihood of bankruptcy for companies.<br>
**4. Provide Early Warnings:** Generate early warnings for stakeholders, enabling them to take proactive measures to mitigate financial distress.<br>
**5. Improve Decision-Making:** Provide insights and recommendations to support informed decision-making for investors, creditors, and other stakeholders.<br>

## 4.	Methodology/ Planning of work

      Methodology Steps

**•	Data Collection:** Gather financial data from company reports, financial statements, and market data sources.<br>
