Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What financial parameters do I need to enter?

To calculate the Altman Z-Score and Ohlson O-Score, you need Total Assets, Current Assets, Current Liabilities, Total Liabilities, Retained Earnings, Sales, EBIT, Net Income, and Operating Cash Flow.

❓ How does the XGBoost model enhance classic scoring?

Classic formulas rely on fixed linear weights from decades ago. Our XGBoost engine analyzes non-linear cross-interactions among liquidity, leverage, and cash-flow trajectories, capturing modern corporate stress patterns.

❓ Can I test sample scenarios without real numbers?

Yes! On the Manual Entry page, we provide instant one-click benchmark presets: Healthy Enterprise, Stressed Retailer, and Critical Distress Manufacturer.
