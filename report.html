📌 Frequently Asked Questions

What statistical models are included in the PDF and Excel reports?

Both reports present Edward Altman's Z-Score (with sub-ratios X1-X5), James Ohlson's 9-factor probabilistic O-Score, and FinShield's calibrated XGBoost Machine Learning classification.

Can I share these reports with external stakeholders?

Yes, both PDF and Excel formats are designed for professional distribution, including board of directors meetings, commercial lenders, bondholders, and auditors.

Is the prediction model explainable?

Yes. The XGBoost model provides feature importance attributions identifying whether distress risk is primarily driven by working capital shortfalls, leverage burdens, or cash flow depletion.
